PROJECT DATA/RESTORE RULES

1. MEGA durable root remains /TelegramBotBackups (or the same configured MEGA_BACKUP_DIR).
2. /restore must restore ALL persistent state contained in the backup scope, not finance rows only.
3. GZ restores complete SQLite/global state. JSON/ISON restores complete global state or complete per-chat saved state according to its scope.
4. pre_restore is mandatory before every manual restore.
5. Per-chat restore must not silently delete unique current records; it performs lossless de-duplication by operation identity.
6. Derived indexes/balances are rebuilt and validated after restore.
7. Tenant/circle security cannot be bypassed by a backup file; a non-platform owner cannot rebind a chat into a foreign tenant.
8. UI/performance changes may not weaken the storage/restore contract.
